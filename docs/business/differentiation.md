# Miles AI — Differentiation

- The only marketing system for home services that gets smarter for every customer because of every other customer.
- **Living playbooks.** Miles ships pre-loaded with proven playbooks across plumbing, HVAC, roofing, garage doors, coatings, windows/doors/siding, and legal. Every campaign outcome writes back into them. The playbook is not documentation — it is the model, and it executes.
- **Cross-account data flywheel.** Learnings promote from account layer to market layer to vertical layer. Account 51 launches with everything accounts 1–50 taught the system.
- **Built on $30M+ in managed spend across 50+ home services accounts.** The cold start is already solved. A funded competitor needs 18 months and real spend to reach parity with day one.
- **Optimizes to booked revenue, not platform metrics.** CRM job outcomes close the loop, so the system learns to buy profitable jobs, not cheap leads.
- **Structural block on all three incumbent types.** Agencies learn in humans — knowledge dies with the account manager. SaaS learns in quarterly roadmaps and cannot act. Horizontal AI agents are contractually barred from cross-account learning; isolation is their core security promise.
- **Depth in the stack that actually runs home services.** Google LSA, CallRail, Yelp, Nextdoor, Yext, ServiceTitan. Operational competence, not a connector count.
- **Purpose-built for the sub-$3M contractor.** No exact-match competitor identified in a ~40-company sweep. Netic and Avoca target larger operators; Scorpion and ServiceTitan serve enterprise.
- **Compounding, not linear.** Every competitor's product is as good on day 500 as day 1 for a new customer. Miles is 500 days smarter.
