# The Miles Covenant — what every customer can expect

> Four promises. Each one is backed by a real mechanism in the product, not a
> slogan. If a promise isn't yet true, we say so plainly rather than imply it is.

Miles serves local-service business owners — people whose money and reputation
are on the line every day. This is what they can expect from us, always.

---

## 1. Trust — we earn the right to act

We never spend, launch, or change anything you didn't allow.

- **You set the leash.** An autonomy dial (10% → 100%) decides how much Miles
  does on its own. Below 100%, every change waits in the **Deploy** queue for
  your **Approve**. At 100%, it acts — and logs everything.
- **Everything is reversible.** The **Change Log** records every change ever
  made. Undo a single task, or roll your whole workspace back to how it was on a
  good day. Your logo and photos are never touched by a rollback.
- **Guardrails on money.** Budget-change caps, "protect proven funnels," and a
  safety floor that keeps money-moving actions off the cheapest AI model — even
  when you've set Miles to save every penny.
- **Your accounts stay yours.** We connect *in* on your behalf through Pipedream.
  We never hold your passwords or your ad spend.
- **Honesty over hype.** We show what's live versus demo. Empty states tell the
  truth; we never fake a number to look impressive.

## 2. Value — we defend every dollar

Miles is judged on your profit, not our output.

- **Profit-optimal AI.** The model router picks the *cheapest* model that clears
  the quality bar for each task. You pay for frontier reasoning only when the job
  needs it.
- **Budget discipline.** Optimizations respect your caps; experiments run where
  they're safe, not on the campaigns already paying your bills.
- **Closed-loop proof.** UTM attribution ties ad spend to real deals, so ROI and
  ROAS are measured, not claimed.
- **Real benchmarks.** Trade benchmarks come from real market data and are
  refreshed — so "good" means good for *your* trade in *your* market.

## 3. Experience — one employee, plain English

Not a toolbox to assemble. An employee that does the work.

- **One surface, whole workflow.** Plan, create, deploy, optimize, report — one
  place, one conversation.
- **Talk normally.** "Add this contact to my CRM," "text that lead," "what's my
  budget doing?" — plain English in, real work out.
- **Works from minute one.** Demo-safe until you connect apps and add keys;
  nothing breaks when it's empty.
- **The details.** Weather triggers, an ad studio that uses your real brand
  assets, a curated integration stack — the small things that make it feel like a
  pro is on the job.

## 4. Expertise — a specialist, not a blank box

Every competitor sells a blank prompt wired to 3,000 apps. We sell the opposite.

- **It already knows your trade.** Vertical command packs — home services,
  roofing, dental, solar — ship knowing your offers, your economics (CPA
  benchmarks, ticket ranges), and your urgency profile.
- **Category-aware plans.** Budget splits and channels are tuned to the trade,
  not generic.
- **The right tools, pre-picked.** The integration stack is curated for your
  industry, so you're not hunting through thousands of apps.

---

## The honesty clause

When a capability isn't built yet, we tell the customer directly — in the
product and in conversation. A promise we can't keep today is named as a promise,
not disguised as a feature. This clause outranks the other four: **we would
rather under-claim and over-deliver than the reverse.**
