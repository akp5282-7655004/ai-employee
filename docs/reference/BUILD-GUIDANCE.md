# Miles campaign build guidance (from Akhil Paul, Nov 21, 2026)

## Sources (all to be committed under docs/reference/)
- ClickMinded SOP library capture (Nov 17): google-ads, paid-acquisition, tracking-measurement SOPs + index.
- ClickMinded SOP 146 "Build a Google Ads Search campaign before launch" (backbone for Google builds).

- Notion "Meta Ads Specialist SOPs" (10 SOPs: setup, audiences, creatives, optimizing, reporting, scaling,
  retargeting, A/B testing, Advantage+, accounts/compliance). Meta baseline. Verify against current Meta docs.
- Akhil's agency checklists (Google Drive "Checklist" folder): PPC, Social Media Paid, Social Media Organic,
  SEO, Google Business Profile, Website, Website Maintenance, plus SEO Writing Checklist.
- TikTok: no SOP; use official TikTok Ads docs + repo specs + trade playbooks.

## Rule: checklists are reference, NOT architecture
- Tier numbers (1/5/10 campaigns, 5/10/20 ad groups, 50/100/200 keywords, 0/1/3 A/B variations,
  Starter/Growth/Scale) are agency package scope limits. Never enforce tiers or tier caps in Miles builds.
- Use checklists only as the list of elements a complete build includes.
- Structure and counts come from the SOPs, trade playbooks, the owner's services, locations and budget.

## Google build must include
Branded campaign plus service campaigns; keyword competition analysis; negative keyword management;
ad headline/description creation and testing (RSA variants); bid management; retargeting/display where it fits;
landing page per ad group; weekly optimization checks; monthly performance report.

## Meta and TikTok builds must include
Strategy summary; competitor research input; campaign creation; creatives; geo targeting; budget management;
A/B testing (one variable at a time, variant count by budget and SOP, not tier); retargeting;
lead form or landing page; weekly optimization checks; monthly performance report.
Meta lead-gen defaults from Notion SOPs: Leads objective, CBO, Lowest Cost then Cost Cap, Advantage+ placements,
1% lookalike then 2-5%, refresh custom audiences every 30-60 days, overlap under 20%, frequency >3 fatigue / >5 refresh
creative, scale 10-20% every 48h, A/B 5-7 days equal budgets. Prefer playbook CPL targets over ROAS/DPA/catalog rules.

## Not part of ad builds
SEO, SEO Writing, GBP, Organic Social, Website, Website Maintenance checklists: stored for future Miles features.
