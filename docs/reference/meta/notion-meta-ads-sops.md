## Setting Up a Meta Ads Campaign


#### Purpose
This SOP outlines the step-by-step process for setting up a successful Meta Ads campaign, ensuring all configurations align with business objectives and best practices.

#### Scope
This SOP applies to all Meta Ads Specialists responsible for planning, setting up, and launching paid ad campaigns across Meta platforms, including Facebook and Instagram.

#### Reference SOPs
- SOP 2: Audience Targeting & Custom Audiences Setup
- SOP 3: Creating High-Converting Ad Creatives
- SOP 4: Optimizing Meta Ads for Performance
- SOP 5: Meta Ads Reporting & Analysis

#### Steps

#### 1. Define Campaign Objectives
- Navigate to Meta Ads Manager and click Create.
- Select the appropriate campaign objective based on the business goal:
  - Awareness: Brand awareness, reach.
  - Consideration: Traffic, engagement, video views, lead generation, messages.
  - Conversion: Conversions, catalog sales, store traffic.
- Ensure the objective aligns with the sales funnel stage.

#### 2. Set Campaign Budget & Bidding Strategy
- Choose between Daily Budget (spends set amount per day) or Lifetime Budget (spends over a set period).
- Select the bidding strategy:
  - Lowest Cost: Best for cost efficiency.
  - Bid Cap: Manual control over bid limits.
  - Cost Cap: Controls the average cost per action.
- If running A/B testing, allocate equal budgets to different ad sets.

#### 3. Configure Ad Set Level Settings
- Audience Selection: Choose target audience (see SOP 2 for details).
- Placement Selection:
  - Automatic Placements (recommended for best optimization).
  - Manual Placements (if targeting specific platforms like Facebook News Feed, Instagram Stories, etc.).
- Optimization & Delivery:
  - Set optimization goal (e.g., link clicks, landing page views, conversions).
  - Choose attribution settings (e.g., 7-day click, 1-day view).

#### 4. Create & Upload Ad Creatives
- Click Create Ad and select the format:
  - Image Ad (single image).
  - Video Ad (short-form or long-form videos).
  - Carousel Ad (multiple images or videos in one ad).
  - Slideshow Ad (looping video using images).
  - Collection Ad (best for eCommerce).
- Add Primary Text, Headline, and Description following best practices (see SOP 3 for details).
- Select the Call-to-Action (CTA) button (e.g., “Shop Now,” “Learn More”).

#### 5. Set Up Tracking & Attribution
- Add the Landing Page URL ensuring UTM parameters are in place for tracking.
- Enable Facebook Pixel tracking (ensure the pixel is properly installed on the website).
- If using Conversions API (CAPI), verify proper configuration for server-side tracking.
- Set up offline event tracking if applicable.

#### 6. Review & Publish
- Double-check all campaign settings, including:
  - Budget & Bidding Strategy
  - Audience Targeting
  - Placements
  - Creatives & Copy
  - Tracking & Attribution
- Click Publish and monitor the campaign closely in the first 24–48 hours.

#### 7. Post-Launch Monitoring & Adjustments
- Monitor ad performance under Ads Manager > Campaigns.
- Check Key Metrics:
  - CTR (Click-Through Rate)
  - CPM (Cost per 1,000 Impressions)
  - CPC (Cost per Click)
  - ROAS (Return on Ad Spend)
  - Frequency (ad fatigue risk)
- Make necessary optimizations based on initial data (see SOP 4 for optimization strategies).

## Audience Targeting & Custom Audiences Setu


#### Purpose
This SOP outlines how to define, create, and refine audience targeting for Meta Ads to maximize ad relevance, improve conversion rates, and minimize ad spend waste.

#### Scope
This SOP applies to all Meta Ads Specialists responsible for building and optimizing audience targeting strategies for Facebook and Instagram ad campaigns.

#### Reference SOPs
- SOP 1: Setting Up a Meta Ads Campaign
- SOP 4: Optimizing Meta Ads for Performance
- SOP 5: Meta Ads Reporting & Analysis

#### Steps

#### 1. Understand Audience Types in Meta Ads
Meta offers multiple audience targeting options. Selecting the right one depends on campaign objectives and customer insights.
- Core Audiences: Target users based on demographics, interests, and behaviors.
- Custom Audiences: Retarget users based on website activity, customer lists, or engagement.
- Lookalike Audiences: Find new users similar to an existing audience.
---

#### 2. Creating a Core Audience (Interest & Demographic Targeting)
- Go to Meta Ads Manager > Audiences.
- Click Create Audience > Saved Audience.
- Define targeting criteria:
  - Location: Country, region, city, radius (e.g., 10-mile radius around a store).
  - Age & Gender: Set according to target market needs.
  - Languages: Only select if targeting non-default languages.
  - Interests & Behaviors: Use detailed targeting for specific industries, behaviors, or competitor interests.
  - Connections: Choose to include/exclude people who already engage with your brand (e.g., “Exclude people who liked our Facebook page”).
- Click Save Audience for future use.
---

#### 3. Creating Custom Audiences for Retargeting
Custom Audiences are used to re-engage previous visitors or customers.
- Navigate to Meta Ads Manager > Audiences.
- Click Create Audience > Custom Audience.
- Choose a source:
  - Website Traffic: Users who visited your site (requires Meta Pixel setup).
  - Customer List: Upload CSV file of existing customers (requires email or phone numbers).
  - Engagement: People who engaged with your Facebook/Instagram page or watched a video.
  - App Activity: Retarget users based on in-app behavior.
- Define conditions (e.g., last 30 days visitors).
- Name the audience and click Create.
---

#### 4. Creating Lookalike Audiences to Expand Reach
Lookalike Audiences help find new customers similar to your existing ones.
- Go to Meta Ads Manager > Audiences.
- Click Create Audience > Lookalike Audience.
- Select a Custom Audience or Customer List as the data source.
- Choose the Lookalike Percentage:
  - 1% (Most Similar) – Best for high-quality targeting.
  - 2%-5% (Broader Reach) – Expands audience size but lowers similarity.
- Select Location (country-specific targeting).
- Click Create Audience.
---

#### 5. Testing & Optimizing Audience Performance
To ensure effective audience targeting:
- Run A/B tests: Test multiple audience variations (e.g., interest-based vs. lookalike).
- Monitor audience overlap: Avoid redundancy by using Audience Overlap Tool in Meta Business Suite.
- Refine based on data: Exclude high-frequency users to prevent ad fatigue.
- Update Lookalikes & Custom Audiences regularly: Refresh every 30-60 days for better performance.

## Creating High-Converting Ad Creatives


#### Purpose
This SOP outlines the process for designing, testing, and optimizing high-performing ad creatives for Meta Ads. It ensures that ads capture attention, drive engagement, and maximize conversions.

#### Scope
This SOP applies to all Meta Ads Specialists responsible for crafting and testing ad creatives, including static images, videos, carousels, and copy.

#### Reference SOPs
- SOP 1: Setting Up a Meta Ads Campaign
- SOP 2: Audience Targeting & Custom Audiences Setup
- SOP 4: Optimizing Meta Ads for Performance
---

#### Steps

#### 1. Choose the Right Ad Format
Meta Ads offers multiple formats. Select the best one based on campaign objectives:
- Image Ads: Best for brand awareness and direct response (simple yet effective).
- Video Ads: High engagement, ideal for storytelling or product demonstrations.
- Carousel Ads: Show multiple images/videos in a single ad (great for eCommerce).
- Collection Ads: Mobile-focused shopping experience with instant store integration.
- Slideshow Ads: Lightweight video alternatives (useful for slow internet speeds).
---

#### 2. Craft an Attention-Grabbing Visual Design
To create engaging ad creatives, follow these best practices:
- Use High-Resolution Images & Videos: Minimum 1080x1080 pixels for images and 4:5 ratio for vertical videos.
- Follow Meta's Text Rule: Avoid too much text overlay; use less than 20% text in images.
- Incorporate Branding: Place logo subtly in a corner but ensure it’s visible.
- Use Contrasting Colors & Bold Elements: High contrast makes ads stand out in the feed.
- Show the Product in Action: Demonstrate how the product is used rather than just showcasing it.
---

#### 3. Write Compelling Ad Copy
Effective ad copy encourages users to take action. Follow these principles:
- Hook in the First Few Words: The first 3-5 words should capture attention (e.g., "Struggling with [problem]?").
- Keep It Concise: Aim for 125 characters or less in primary text.
- Use Simple, Conversational Language: Avoid jargon; write like you're speaking to a friend.
- Highlight Benefits Over Features: Instead of “Our shoes have memory foam,” say “Walk all day without foot pain.”
- Include a Strong Call-to-Action (CTA): Examples:
  - “Shop Now” for eCommerce.
  - “Sign Up” for lead generation.
  - “Get Offer” for promotions.
---

#### 4. Use Meta’s Dynamic Creative Feature
Dynamic Creatives automatically test multiple ad variations to find the best performer.
- In Ads Manager, select Create Ad and turn on Dynamic Creative.
- Upload multiple versions of:
  - Images/videos
  - Headlines
  - Primary text
  - CTA buttons
- Meta will mix and match elements to optimize ad performance in real time.
---

#### 5. Implement A/B Testing for Creative Performance
To determine the most effective creative, run A/B tests:
- Test One Variable at a Time: Examples:
  - Image A vs. Image B
  - Short copy vs. long copy
  - Video vs. static image
- Monitor Key Metrics:
  - CTR (Click-Through Rate): Measures engagement.
  - CPC (Cost Per Click): Determines ad efficiency.
  - Conversion Rate: Indicates how well an ad drives results.
- Optimize Based on Data: Pause underperforming creatives and scale winning ones.
---

#### 6. Ensure Compliance with Meta’s Ad Policies
Before launching, review Meta’s Advertising Policies to avoid disapproval:
- No misleading claims (e.g., “Lose 10 lbs in a week!”).
- No excessive before-and-after images (especially for fitness/beauty).
- No personal attributes targeting (e.g., “Are you depressed?”).
- No low-quality ads (e.g., blurry images, excessive text).
---

#### 7. Monitor & Refresh Ad Creatives Regularly
- Rotate Creatives Every 2-4 Weeks: Prevents ad fatigue.
- Analyze Performance Trends: Check Ad Relevance Diagnostics in Ads Manager.
- Repurpose Top-Performing Creatives: Turn winning static images into slideshows/videos for fresh engagement.

## Optimizing Meta Ads for Performance


#### Purpose
This SOP provides a structured approach to optimizing Meta Ads campaigns for better performance, ensuring maximum return on ad spend (ROAS) and efficient ad delivery.

#### Scope
This SOP applies to all Meta Ads Specialists responsible for ongoing performance monitoring, testing, and optimization of active campaigns.

#### Reference SOPs
- SOP 1: Setting Up a Meta Ads Campaign
- SOP 2: Audience Targeting & Custom Audiences Setup
- SOP 3: Creating High-Converting Ad Creatives
- SOP 5: Meta Ads Reporting & Analysis
---

#### Steps

#### 1. Establish Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
Before optimizing, define the key success metrics based on campaign objectives:
- Brand Awareness:
  - Reach
  - Impressions
  - Frequency
- Traffic & Engagement:
  - Click-Through Rate (CTR)
  - Cost Per Click (CPC)
  - Engagement Rate
- Lead Generation & Sales:
  - Cost Per Lead (CPL)
  - Conversion Rate
  - Return on Ad Spend (ROAS)
- E-commerce Metrics:
  - Cost Per Purchase
  - Average Order Value (AOV)
  - Cart Abandonment Rate
---

#### 2. Analyze Ad Performance in Meta Ads Manager
To determine optimization areas:
- Go to Meta Ads Manager > Campaigns.
- Check Performance Metrics:
  - Low CTR (<1%) → Ad creative or audience mismatch.
  - High CPC → Improve ad relevance or bidding strategy.
  - Low Conversion Rate → Landing page issues or weak CTA.
  - High Frequency (>3-5) → Ad fatigue (requires new creatives or audience expansion).
---

#### 3. Optimize Audience Targeting
- Refine Custom Audiences:
  - Exclude past purchasers from conversion campaigns.
  - Segment warm audiences (e.g., page engagers vs. website visitors).
- Adjust Lookalike Audiences:
  - Test 1% vs. 2-5% Lookalikes to balance precision and scale.
  - Use value-based Lookalikes for eCommerce to find high-value customers.
- Monitor Audience Overlap:
  - In Audience Manager, check for overlapping audiences (reduces competition & costs).
  - If overlap is >20%, consolidate or exclude overlapping groups.
---

#### 4. Improve Ad Creatives & Copy
- A/B Test Variations:
  - Test different headlines, images, videos, and CTAs.
  - Keep the best-performing ads and pause underperforming ones.
- Use Dynamic Creative Optimization (DCO):
  - Turn on Dynamic Creative to let Meta test multiple combinations automatically.
- Rotate Ad Creatives Regularly:
  - Change creatives every 2-4 weeks to avoid ad fatigue.
  - Refresh ads with new offers, messaging, or visuals.
---

#### 5. Optimize Bidding & Budget Allocation
- Use Lowest Cost Bidding (Auto Bidding) for cost-effective scaling.
- Increase Budget for Winning Campaigns:
  - If ROAS > 3X, scale by 10-20% daily to maintain stability.
- Reduce Spend on Poor Performers:
  - If ROAS < 1X, adjust targeting or pause the campaign.
- Shift Budget to High-Converting Placements:
  - Use Breakdown > Placement Report to analyze performance.
  - If Instagram Stories outperform Facebook Feed, shift more budget there.
---

#### 6. Optimize Ad Delivery & Placement
- Enable Advantage+ Placements (formerly Automatic Placements) for better efficiency.
- Manually Optimize if Needed:
  - Remove underperforming placements (e.g., Audience Network).
  - Allocate budget toward highest CTR placements.
- Adjust Attribution Window:
  - Shorten from 7-day click to 1-day click for faster data.
  - Extend for high-ticket items with a longer buying cycle.
---

#### 7. Reduce Wasted Ad Spend
- Use Retargeting Exclusions:
  - Prevent showing ads to past purchasers (unless upselling).
  - Exclude users who already engaged but didn’t convert after 30 days.
- Set Frequency Caps:
  - If Frequency >5, adjust audience size or refresh creatives.
- Monitor Audience Saturation:
  - If CTR drops & CPM rises, the audience is likely saturated.
  - Expand audience size or create fresh Lookalikes.
---

#### 8. Implement Automated Rules for Efficiency
- In Meta Ads Manager, go to Rules and set automation for:
  - Pause ads if CPA exceeds X amount.
  - Increase budget if ROAS is above X.
  - Send notification if CTR drops below X.
---

#### 9. Monitor Performance & Adjust Weekly
- Daily: Check budget pacing & anomalies.
- Weekly: Adjust creatives, targeting, and placements.
- Monthly: Review big-picture trends and shift strategy accordingly.

## Meta Ads Reporting & Analysis


#### Purpose
This SOP outlines the process for generating, analyzing, and interpreting Meta Ads performance reports. It ensures data-driven decision-making to optimize future campaigns and improve return on ad spend (ROAS).

#### Scope
This SOP applies to all Meta Ads Specialists responsible for tracking, measuring, and reporting ad campaign performance across Meta platforms.

#### Reference SOPs
- SOP 1: Setting Up a Meta Ads Campaign
- SOP 2: Audience Targeting & Custom Audiences Setup
- SOP 3: Creating High-Converting Ad Creatives
- SOP 4: Optimizing Meta Ads for Performance
---

#### Steps

#### 1. Define Reporting Goals & KPIs
Before generating reports, establish key performance indicators (KPIs) based on the campaign objective:
- Brand Awareness Campaigns:
  - Impressions
  - Reach
  - Frequency
  - Cost per 1,000 Impressions (CPM)
- Traffic & Engagement Campaigns:
  - Click-Through Rate (CTR)
  - Cost Per Click (CPC)
  - Engagement Rate
- Lead Generation & Sales Campaigns:
  - Cost Per Lead (CPL)
  - Conversion Rate
  - Cost Per Purchase
  - Return on Ad Spend (ROAS)
---

#### 2. Access Meta Ads Manager Reports
- Navigate to Meta Ads Manager > Campaigns.
- Click Customize Columns to display relevant metrics.
- Use Breakdown Filters (e.g., by age, gender, placement) to gain deeper insights.
---

#### 3. Analyze Campaign-Level Performance
- Look at Overall Campaign ROAS:
  - If ROAS is above 3X, consider scaling the budget.
  - If ROAS is below 1X, reassess targeting, creatives, and landing pages.
- Compare Budget & Spend Efficiency:
  - Ensure the campaign is spending within allocated limits.
  - If spend is too low, adjust budget pacing or increase bid caps.
---

#### 4. Evaluate Ad Set-Level Performance
- Check Audience Performance:
  - Identify which audience segments are driving conversions.
  - Pause underperforming audience segments.
- Analyze Placement Effectiveness:
  - Use Breakdown > Placement to determine best-performing ad placements.
  - Shift budget toward high-performing placements.
- Monitor Frequency & Ad Fatigue:
  - If Frequency >5, refresh creatives or expand the audience.
---

#### 5. Review Ad-Level Performance
- Analyze Creative Effectiveness:
  - Compare CTR, engagement, and conversion rates across different ad creatives.
  - Identify winning creatives and scale them.
- Check Ad Copy & CTA Performance:
  - Compare different ad texts and CTA buttons.
  - Optimize underperforming variations.
---

#### 6. Track Landing Page Performance
- Check Website Analytics:
  - Compare Landing Page Views vs. Link Clicks (low ratio indicates slow site speed or irrelevant traffic).
  - Use Google Analytics UTM tracking to verify conversion paths.
- Review Bounce Rate & Time on Page:
  - If bounce rate is above 70%, check for slow page speed or poor page relevance.
  - Optimize for mobile-first experiences.
---

#### 7. Generate & Export Reports
- In Ads Manager, click Reports > Export Data.
- Choose preferred format (Excel, CSV, or PDF).
- Create Automated Reports:
  - Go to Ads Reporting > Create Scheduled Report.
  - Select frequency (Daily, Weekly, Monthly).
  - Email the report to stakeholders.
---

#### 8. Provide Insights & Recommendations
- Summarize Key Findings:
  - What worked well?
  - What needs improvement?
- Suggest Next Steps:
  - Optimize targeting, bidding, or creative strategies based on findings.
- Use Visual Data Representation:
  - Include graphs/charts to simplify performance insights.
---

#### 9. Present the Report to Stakeholders
- Schedule Weekly/Monthly Performance Meetings.
- Highlight Actionable Insights.
- Align Future Strategies Based on Data.

## Scaling Meta Ad Campaigns Successfully


#### Purpose
This SOP provides a structured approach for scaling Meta Ads campaigns efficiently while maintaining profitability, avoiding ad fatigue, and optimizing return on ad spend (ROAS).

#### Scope
This SOP applies to all Meta Ads Specialists responsible for increasing budget, audience reach, and overall ad performance while ensuring ad stability.

#### Reference SOPs
- SOP 1: Setting Up a Meta Ads Campaign
- SOP 4: Optimizing Meta Ads for Performance
- SOP 5: Meta Ads Reporting & Analysis
---

#### Steps

#### 1. Determine If a Campaign is Ready to Scale
Before scaling, confirm that the campaign meets the following criteria:
- Consistent Performance:
  - ROAS is at least 3X for eCommerce.
  - Cost Per Lead (CPL) is within the target range for lead generation.
- Sufficient Data:
  - The campaign has received at least 50-100 conversions in the past 7 days.
- Stable Ad Delivery:
  - Ad sets are delivering without major fluctuations in CPC, CPM, or CTR.
- Low Audience Saturation:
  - Frequency <3 (higher frequency may indicate audience fatigue).
---

#### 2. Choose a Scaling Method
Meta Ads can be scaled vertically (increasing budget) or horizontally (expanding reach).

#### A. Vertical Scaling (Budget Increase)
- Slow Budget Increase (Recommended for Stability)
  - Increase budget by 10-20% every 48 hours.
  - Avoid sudden jumps (e.g., from $50/day to $500/day) to prevent re-entering the learning phase.
- Scaling with CBO (Campaign Budget Optimization)
  - Turn on CBO to let Meta distribute budget dynamically across top-performing ad sets.
- Using Automated Rules to Scale Budget
  - Go to Ads Manager > Rules > Create Rule.
  - Set a rule to increase budget by 20% if ROAS > 3X.
  - Set a rule to pause ad sets if CPA exceeds the target amount.

#### B. Horizontal Scaling (Expanding Audience Reach)
- Expand Lookalike Audiences
  - Start with a 1% Lookalike and gradually test 2%-5% Lookalikes to expand reach.
- Test New Audiences
  - Create new ad sets targeting different interest groups, behaviors, and demographics.
  - Use Audience Insights to discover new segments.
- Expand to Different Placements
  - Enable Advantage+ Placements (automatic placements) for wider reach.
  - If Facebook Feed performs best, try Instagram Stories & Reels for additional reach.
- Run Ads in Multiple Languages & Regions
  - Localize ads for different markets (if applicable).
  - Test campaigns in new geographic locations.
---

#### 3. Optimize Ads for Scaling Success
- Refresh Creatives Regularly
  - Rotate new creatives every 2-4 weeks to prevent ad fatigue.
  - Repurpose high-performing creatives by adjusting text, format, or background.
- Leverage Video & Dynamic Ads
  - Video ads often scale better than static images.
  - Use Dynamic Creative Ads to test multiple variations at once.
- Adjust Bidding Strategy for Higher Spending
  - Switch from Lowest Cost (Auto Bidding) to Cost Cap or Bid Cap to maintain efficiency.
  - Set a Bid Cap slightly above your profitable CPA to stay competitive.
---

#### 4. Monitor Performance During Scaling
- Daily Check-ins:
  - Ensure CPC, CTR, and CPM remain stable.
  - Look for signs of audience fatigue (rising CPM, decreasing CTR).
- Weekly Deep-Dive:
  - Compare before and after scaling results in Ads Manager.
  - Identify what’s working and adjust underperforming ads.
- Adjust Budget Allocation:
  - Shift more budget toward high-performing ad sets.
  - Reduce spend on ad sets with a declining ROAS.
---

#### 5. Use Retargeting for Sustainable Scaling
- Segment Retargeting Audiences
  - Exclude users who have already converted.
  - Retarget visitors who abandoned carts or engaged with previous ads.
- Use Dynamic Product Ads (DPA)
  - If running an eCommerce store, use DPAs to show personalized product recommendations to past visitors.
- Create Sequential Ad Campaigns
  - Serve different ads to users at different stages of the funnel.
  - Example: Show a video ad first, then a product-focused ad to engagers.
---

#### 6. Avoid Common Scaling Mistakes
- Scaling Too Fast:
  - Doubling the budget overnight can reset the learning phase and reduce efficiency.
- Ignoring Ad Fatigue:
  - High frequency leads to declining CTR—introduce new creatives regularly.
- Overlapping Audiences:
  - Use Audience Overlap Tool to avoid competing against your own ads.
- Not Monitoring Performance Closely:
  - Scaling requires daily monitoring to ensure costs don’t spike unexpectedly.
---

#### 7. Plan for Long-Term Scaling Success
- Develop a Content Calendar:
  - Plan new ad creatives in advance.
  - Schedule seasonal promotions & testing cycles.
- Test & Adapt New Strategies:
  - Experiment with AI-powered Advantage+ campaigns for automated scaling.
  - Stay updated with Meta’s algorithm changes and ad platform updates.
- Reinvest Profits into New Campaigns:
  - Allocate part of your budget for testing new ad formats & audience segments.

## Retargeting Strategies for Maximum Conversions


#### Purpose
This SOP outlines how to implement high-converting Meta Ads retargeting strategies to re-engage past visitors, leads, and customers, increasing overall conversion rates.

#### Scope
This SOP applies to all Meta Ads Specialists responsible for designing and managing retargeting campaigns across Facebook and Instagram.

#### Reference SOPs
- SOP 2: Audience Targeting & Custom Audiences Setup
- SOP 4: Optimizing Meta Ads for Performance
- SOP 6: Scaling Meta Ad Campaigns Successfully
---

#### Steps

#### 1. Understand the Types of Retargeting Audiences
Meta allows retargeting based on user behavior. The most effective retargeting audiences include:
- Website Visitors (via Meta Pixel)
  - Users who visited your site but didn’t take action.
  - Segment by specific pages (e.g., product page viewers vs. cart abandoners).
- Engagement-Based Retargeting
  - Users who interacted with your Instagram or Facebook page.
  - Video viewers (e.g., 50%, 75%, or 95% watch time).
- Customer List Retargeting
  - Retarget email subscribers, past customers, or leads from a CRM upload.
- Lookalike Retargeting
  - Find new audiences similar to high-value past customers.
---

#### 2. Set Up Custom Audiences for Retargeting
- Go to Meta Ads Manager > Audiences.
- Click Create Audience > Custom Audience.
- Choose the data source:
  - Website Traffic → Select “People who visited specific web pages” and set a time frame (e.g., last 30 days).
  - Engagement → Select “People who engaged with your Facebook or Instagram content.”
  - Customer List → Upload an email list from your CRM (must include hashed email/phone numbers).
- Click Create and save for later use in ad sets.
---

#### 3. Create a Retargeting Funnel Based on Buyer Journey
Segment your audience based on their engagement level:

#### A. Top-of-Funnel Retargeting (TOF) – Engagers
- Target users who watched a video, liked a post, or clicked on an ad but didn’t visit the website.
- Ad Type: Brand reminder video, testimonials, or carousel ads.
- Message: Soft pitch, brand credibility, education.
- Example CTA: “Learn More” or “See Why Thousands Love Us.”

#### B. Middle-of-Funnel Retargeting (MOF) – Website Visitors
- Target users who visited your website but didn’t take action.
- Ad Type: Product-focused image/video ads, social proof (reviews).
- Message: Emphasize value propositions, limited-time offers.
- Example CTA: “Check Out What’s New” or “Exclusive Offer Inside.”

#### C. Bottom-of-Funnel Retargeting (BOF) – Cart Abandoners & Leads
- Target users who added to cart, started checkout, or signed up but didn’t convert.
- Ad Type: Dynamic product ads (DPA), urgency-driven ads.
- Message: “Complete your order before the deal expires!”
- Example CTA: “Get 10% Off Your First Order” or “Claim Your Offer.”
---

#### 4. Set Up Dynamic Retargeting Ads (DPA) for eCommerce
Dynamic Product Ads automatically show users the exact product they viewed.
- Go to Meta Commerce Manager and ensure your product catalog is uploaded.
- Create a Catalog Sales Campaign in Ads Manager.
- Choose Dynamic Ads > Retarget People Who Viewed Products But Didn’t Buy.
- Select a time frame (e.g., last 14-30 days).
- Optimize ad creatives:
  - Auto-populate product images, pricing, and details.
  - Add overlays like “Limited Stock!” to drive urgency.
---

#### 5. Optimize Ad Frequency to Prevent Fatigue
- Check Ad Frequency Metrics in Ads Manager.
  - Ideal Frequency: 1.5 - 3 (avoid exceeding 5).
- Use Frequency Caps in ad settings.
- Rotate Creative Every 2 Weeks:
  - Use different angles: Social proof, testimonials, urgency-based messaging.
  - Alternate between images, videos, and carousel formats.
---

#### 6. Create Time-Based Exclusions to Improve Efficiency
- Exclude Users Who Already Purchased:
  - Go to Audience Exclusions in Ads Manager.
  - Select Customer List > Past Buyers (Last 30 Days).
- Exclude Non-Engaged Visitors (if they didn’t take action within 60 days).
- Exclude Duplicate Retargeting Segments:
  - If running multiple retargeting campaigns, ensure audience overlap is below 20%.
---

#### 7. A/B Test Retargeting Ads for Maximum Conversions
- Test Different Messaging:
  - Emotional appeal (e.g., “Don’t Miss Out!”) vs. Logical appeal (e.g., “Limited-Time Offer”).
- Test Offers & Incentives:
  - Free shipping vs. 10% discount.
- Test CTA Buttons:
  - “Shop Now” vs. “Claim Your Offer.”
Monitor results in Ads Manager > Breakdown by Ad and adjust accordingly.
---

#### 8. Use Lookalike Audiences to Expand Retargeting Success
- Create 1% Lookalike Audience from:
  - High-value customers.
  - Cart abandoners who returned and completed a purchase.
- Test 2-5% Lookalikes to expand reach gradually.
---

#### 9. Automate Retargeting with Meta’s AI-Powered Tools
- Enable Advantage+ Audience Expansion for AI-driven optimizations.
- Use Automated Rules to pause underperforming ads:
  - Set a rule to stop an ad if CTR drops below 1%.
  - Increase budget if ROAS exceeds 4X.
---

#### 10. Review & Optimize Weekly
- Check Ad Fatigue & Frequency Levels.
- Analyze ROAS & Cost Per Result Trends.
- Adjust Budget Allocation Based on Winning Ad Sets.

## A/B Testing Strategies for Meta Ads


#### Purpose
This SOP outlines the process for conducting A/B tests (split tests) in Meta Ads to determine which ad elements drive the best performance, leading to higher conversions and better return on ad spend (ROAS).

#### Scope
This SOP applies to all Meta Ads Specialists responsible for optimizing ad creatives, targeting, bidding strategies, and placements through structured A/B testing.

#### Reference SOPs
- SOP 3: Creating High-Converting Ad Creatives
- SOP 4: Optimizing Meta Ads for Performance
- SOP 5: Meta Ads Reporting & Analysis
---

#### Steps

#### 1. Define the Objective of the A/B Test
Before running a test, identify what you want to improve:
- Ad Creatives: Which image or video performs better?
- Ad Copy & Messaging: Which headline or primary text converts more users?
- Audience Targeting: Which audience segment has a higher ROAS?
- Placements: Does Facebook Feed perform better than Instagram Stories?
- Bidding Strategies: Which bidding option results in lower costs?
---

#### 2. Set Up a Structured A/B Test in Meta Ads Manager
Meta provides a built-in A/B testing tool:
- Go to Ads Manager > Experiments.
- Click A/B Test > Create A/B Test.
- Select the variable you want to test (audience, creative, placement, etc.).
- Duplicate the original campaign/ad set and modify only one element.
---

#### 3. Best Practices for Setting Up A/B Tests
- Test One Variable at a Time
  - Example: If testing ad copy, keep the image, targeting, and budget the same.
- Ensure a Large Enough Sample Size
  - Each ad set should reach at least 1,000+ users before drawing conclusions.
- Use the Same Budget for Each Variation
  - Prevent bias by keeping budgets identical across test groups.
- Let the Test Run for At Least 5-7 Days
  - Meta’s learning phase requires time to stabilize results.
---

#### 4. Testing Ad Creatives & Copy

#### A. Image vs. Video
- Create two ads: one using a static image, the other using a video.
- Compare CTR, engagement, and conversion rate.

#### B. Different Headlines & Primary Text
- Keep the image/video the same but change the headline and primary text.
- Example:
  - Headline A: "Boost Your Productivity with This Simple Tool!"
  - Headline B: "Struggling with Time Management? Try This Now!"

#### C. Testing Call-to-Action (CTA) Buttons
- Test “Shop Now” vs. “Learn More” to see which drives more conversions.
---

#### 5. Testing Audience Targeting

#### A. Interest-Based vs. Lookalike Audiences
- Create one ad set targeting detailed interests (e.g., competitors’ brands, industry-specific behaviors).
- Create another ad set using a 1% Lookalike Audience from past purchasers.
- Compare CPC, CTR, and ROAS.

#### B. Broad Targeting vs. Narrow Targeting
- Test a broad audience (18-65, all interests) vs. a narrow audience (specific age group, location, and behaviors).

#### C. Testing Retargeting Windows
- Run ads targeting website visitors from the last 7 days vs. last 30 days to see which performs better.
---

#### 6. Testing Placements & Ad Delivery

#### A. Facebook Feed vs. Instagram Stories
- Run identical ads but limit one to Facebook Feed and the other to Instagram Stories.
- Analyze CTR and Cost Per Result to determine the best-performing placement.

#### B. Manual Placements vs. Advantage+ Placements
- Let one ad use manual placements and another use Advantage+ (automatic placements).
- Compare how Meta optimizes delivery across different platforms.
---

#### 7. Testing Budget & Bidding Strategies

#### A. Lowest Cost Bidding vs. Cost Cap Bidding
- Use Lowest Cost Bidding in one ad set (automatic bidding).
- Use Cost Cap Bidding in another ad set (set a manual max bid).
- Compare CPL and CPA to determine which bidding strategy is more efficient.

#### B. Daily Budget vs. Lifetime Budget
- Run the same ad with a daily budget in one test and a lifetime budget in another.
- Evaluate which budget setting results in a lower cost per conversion.
---

#### 8. Monitor A/B Test Results in Meta Ads Manager
- Navigate to Ads Manager > Breakdown > A/B Test Results.
- Compare metrics like:
  - CTR (Click-Through Rate)
  - CPC (Cost Per Click)
  - Conversion Rate
  - ROAS (Return on Ad Spend)
---

#### 9. Scale Winning Variations & Apply Learnings
- Pause the losing variation once a winner is determined.
- Scale the winning ad by increasing budget by 10-20% daily.
- Apply successful findings to future campaigns.
---

#### 10. Document A/B Test Learnings for Future Use
- Log Test Results in a Spreadsheet:
  - What worked?
  - What failed?
  - Key takeaways?
- Standardize Best Practices:
  - If a certain ad format consistently outperforms others, use it as the default in future campaigns.

## Using Meta Advantage+ Campaigns for AI-Driven Optimization


#### Purpose
This SOP outlines how to set up, manage, and optimize Meta Advantage+ Campaigns, which use AI-driven automation to improve ad performance with minimal manual intervention.

#### Scope
This SOP applies to all Meta Ads Specialists looking to leverage Meta’s AI-powered Advantage+ solutions to optimize campaign delivery, audience targeting, and budget allocation.

#### Reference SOPs
- SOP 1: Setting Up a Meta Ads Campaign
- SOP 4: Optimizing Meta Ads for Performance
- SOP 6: Scaling Meta Ad Campaigns Successfully
---

#### Steps

#### 1. Understand What Meta Advantage+ Campaigns Are
Meta’s Advantage+ Campaigns use machine learning to optimize ads in real-time. Key features include:
- Advantage+ Shopping Campaigns – Best for eCommerce businesses wanting to automate audience targeting and budget distribution.
- Advantage+ Audience Targeting – AI refines audience selection beyond manual targeting.
- Advantage+ Placements – Automatically delivers ads to the highest-performing placements (Facebook Feed, Instagram Stories, Reels, etc.).
---

#### 2. Determine When to Use Advantage+ Campaigns
Advantage+ is ideal for:
- E-commerce & lead generation campaigns with existing conversion data.
- Scaling successful campaigns with automation instead of manual adjustments.
- Testing new audience segments without needing manual targeting.
---

#### 3. Setting Up an Advantage+ Shopping Campaign (for E-commerce)

#### A. Create the Campaign
- Go to Meta Ads Manager > Create Campaign.
- Select Sales Objective > Advantage+ Shopping Campaign.

#### B. Configure Campaign Settings
- Set your budget (daily or lifetime).
- Choose your conversion goal (e.g., Purchase or Add to Cart).
- Connect your product catalog if using Dynamic Product Ads (DPA).

#### C. Audience Targeting (Let AI Optimize It)
- Leave the audience broad (Advantage+ will automatically optimize targeting).
- If desired, exclude past buyers to focus on new customers.

#### D. Ad Placements (Automatic Optimization)
- Use Advantage+ Placements (formerly known as Automatic Placements).
- AI will determine where to show ads based on performance.

#### E. Ad Creatives & Copy
- Upload multiple ad variations (images, videos, headlines, primary text).
- Enable Dynamic Creative Optimization (DCO) so Meta can mix & match elements for the best performance.

#### F. Bidding Strategy
- Select Lowest Cost Bidding (recommended for beginners).
- If scaling, use Cost Cap to control spending per purchase.

#### G. Publish & Monitor Performance
- Click Publish and let the system run for at least 3-5 days before making changes.
---

#### 4. Setting Up an Advantage+ Audience Campaign (for Lead Generation & Traffic Goals)

#### A. Go to Meta Ads Manager > Create a Campaign
- Choose Leads, Traffic, or Awareness as the objective.
- Select Advantage+ Audience to enable AI-driven audience selection.

#### B. Let AI Expand Audience Targeting
- Instead of setting strict interests, let Meta automatically adjust targeting based on ad engagement.
- Exclude unwanted segments (e.g., current customers or unqualified leads).

#### C. Run Multiple Ad Variations
- Upload multiple images, videos, headlines, and descriptions.
- Let Advantage+ test which combinations perform best.
---

#### 5. Monitor & Optimize Advantage+ Campaigns

#### A. Check Key Performance Metrics in Ads Manager
- ROAS (Return on Ad Spend) → If ROAS drops, refresh ad creatives.
- CTR (Click-Through Rate) → If low, test new headlines or visuals.
- CPA (Cost Per Acquisition) → If rising, adjust bid cap or exclusions.

#### B. Allow at Least 5-7 Days for the Learning Phase
- Avoid making changes too soon, as AI needs time to optimize.
- After the learning phase, make small budget adjustments (10-20% at a time).

#### C. Scale Winning Ads with Budget Increases
- Increase the budget gradually if the campaign is performing well.
- Keep ad creative fresh every 2-4 weeks to prevent fatigue.
---

#### 6. Common Mistakes to Avoid with Advantage+ Campaigns
🚫 Changing Settings Too Early – Wait at least 5 days before making adjustments.
🚫 Overloading AI with Too Many Restrictions – Let Meta’s algorithm decide audience & placements.
🚫 Forgetting to Exclude Past Converters – Avoid wasting budget on users who already purchased.
---

#### 7. Automating Advantage+ Campaign Scaling
- Use Automated Rules in Ads Manager:
  - Increase budget by 20% if ROAS > 4X.
  - Pause ads if CPA exceeds a set amount.
- Leverage Meta’s AI Insights for recommendations.
---

#### 8. Reviewing Performance & Adjusting Strategy
- Daily: Monitor budget pacing & conversions.
- Weekly: Identify trends & tweak creatives.
- Monthly: Adjust long-term strategy based on data.

## Managing Meta Ad Accounts & Compliance Best Practices


#### Purpose
This SOP outlines how to properly manage Meta Ad accounts, ensure compliance with Meta’s advertising policies, and prevent account suspensions or bans.

#### Scope
This SOP applies to all Meta Ads Specialists responsible for account security, compliance, and ad policy adherence.

#### Reference SOPs
- SOP 1: Setting Up a Meta Ads Campaign
- SOP 4: Optimizing Meta Ads for Performance
- SOP 5: Meta Ads Reporting & Analysis
---

#### Steps

#### 1. Setting Up & Managing a Meta Business Manager Account

#### A. Create a Business Manager Account
- Go to Meta Business Manager.
- Click Create Account and enter business details.
- Verify your business email and phone number.

#### B. Add & Assign Roles to Team Members
- Navigate to Business Settings > People.
- Click Add People, enter their email, and assign roles:
  - Admin: Full access to business settings.
  - Advertiser: Can create & manage ads but no access to billing.
  - Analyst: Can view reports but cannot create ads.

#### C. Connect Ad Accounts & Payment Methods
- Go to Business Settings > Accounts > Ad Accounts.
- Click Add > Create a New Ad Account or Request Access to an Existing Ad Account.
- Navigate to Payments > Add Payment Method, ensuring a valid credit card or PayPal is linked.
---

#### 2. Ensuring Meta Ad Account Security

#### A. Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)
- Go to Business Settings > Security Center.
- Enable Two-Factor Authentication for all users.

#### B. Set Account Spending Limits
- Navigate to Ad Account Settings > Set Spending Limit to prevent overspending.

#### C. Regularly Review Active Users
- Check Business Settings > People to remove inactive or unauthorized users.

#### D. Use Facebook’s Security Checkup
- Visit Facebook Security Checkup to scan for vulnerabilities.
---

#### 3. Compliance with Meta Ad Policies

#### A. Review Meta’s Ad Policies Before Launching Campaigns
- Familiarize yourself with Meta’s Ad Policies to avoid disapproved ads.
- Key compliance areas:
  - Prohibited Content – No misleading claims, adult content, or illegal products.
  - Restricted Content – Alcohol, financial services, and healthcare ads require approval.
  - No Clickbait or Sensational Content – Avoid misleading images or exaggerated headlines.

#### B. Ensure Landing Page Compliance
- The landing page must:
  - Match the ad content.
  - Have a privacy policy (if collecting leads).
  - Be mobile-optimized.

#### C. Avoid Personal Attributes in Ad Copy
🚫 Incorrect: “Are you struggling with weight loss?”
✅ Correct: “This new fitness program has helped thousands achieve their goals!”

#### D. Stay Within the 20% Text Rule for Ad Images
- Use Meta’s Text Overlay Tool to check compliance.
---

#### 4. Preventing & Resolving Account Suspensions

#### A. Common Reasons for Ad Account Suspension
- Violating Ad Policies (misleading claims, restricted content).
- Excessive Disapprovals (too many rejected ads).
- Unusual Payment Activity (declined transactions).
- Duplicate Ad Accounts violating Business Manager policies.

#### B. Steps to Prevent Account Bans
- Keep rejection rates below 10% by following policy guidelines.
- Avoid sudden large budget increases (Meta flags suspicious activity).
- Regularly update payment methods to prevent billing issues.

#### C. Steps to Recover a Suspended Account
- Go to Meta’s Account Quality Center.
- Submit an appeal by clicking Request Review.
- Provide supporting documents (business verification, ID proof).
- If needed, contact Meta Business Support via Meta Business Help Center.
---

#### 5. Managing Multiple Ad Accounts Without Getting Banned

#### A. Follow Meta’s Account Structure Best Practices
- One Business Manager Per Legal Entity.
- One Ad Account Per Business Unit (unless scaling requires multiple accounts).
- Avoid Running Ads from New Accounts with No History.

#### B. Using Agency or Client Ad Accounts Safely
- Request Partner Access Instead of Using Client Credentials.
- Never Log In to Multiple Accounts from Different IPs (Can Trigger Suspensions).
- Keep a Clean Payment History to Avoid Flagging.
---

#### 6. Managing Billing & Payments in Meta Ads

#### A. Adding & Managing Payment Methods
- Go to Business Settings > Payments.
- Click Add Payment Method and select Credit Card, PayPal, or Direct Debit.
- Set a Primary Payment Method and a Backup Payment Method (prevents disruptions).

#### B. Setting Up Monthly Invoicing (For High-Spending Accounts)
- Apply for Meta Monthly Invoicing if spending $10,000+/month.
- Contact Meta Business Support to request an invoicing plan.

#### C. Downloading Invoices for Accounting
- Go to Business Settings > Transactions.
- Select Date Range > Download Invoice (PDF or CSV).
---

#### 7. Monitoring & Resolving Ad Disapprovals

#### A. Steps to Appeal a Disapproved Ad
- In Ads Manager, find the rejected ad and click Edit.
- Click Request Review to appeal the decision.
- If rejected again, check for policy violations and adjust ad copy, creative, or targeting.

#### B. Contacting Meta Support for Urgent Issues
- Visit Meta Business Help Center.
- Click Get Support > Select Chat or Email Support (available for eligible accounts).
- Provide Ad ID, account details, and a clear explanation of the issue.
---

#### 8. Conducting Regular Ad Account Audits

#### A. Monthly Audit Checklist
✅ Review active campaigns for compliance.
✅ Check ad performance & disapproval rates.
✅ Verify account security settings (2FA enabled, no unauthorized users).
✅ Ensure payment methods are up to date.

#### B. Quarterly Business Manager Audit
- Remove inactive users from Business Settings.
- Review spending limits & budgets for accuracy.
- Check for billing inconsistencies or policy warnings.
---

#### 9. Keeping Up with Meta’s Policy & Algorithm Updates

#### A. Follow Official Meta Channels
- Meta Business Blog: https://www.facebook.com/business/news
- Meta Ads Help Center: https://www.facebook.com/business/help
- Meta for Developers (API & Tracking Changes): https://developers.facebook.com/

#### B. Join Meta Ads Communities
- Facebook Blueprint Training Courses (free certification courses).
- Join Meta Ads Specialist Groups for latest updates & strategies.
---

#### 10. Establishing a Meta Ads SOP & Compliance Process
- Train team members on ad compliance & account security.
- Keep a shared compliance document with updated policy guidelines.
- Run quarterly compliance reviews to prevent unexpected bans.
